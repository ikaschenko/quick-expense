
If you track your personal expenses systematically, you're probably doing it in Excel or Google Sheets. Great on a PC. Not always mobile-friendly. That's exactly why I built QuickExpense. It's mobile-first — log an expense from your phone in a free minute, waiting in line, or when you're just too lazy to open a laptop. And here's the key point: your data stays in a Google Sheet you own and control, just like before. QuickExpense just adds a cozy UI on top.

Let's see how it works.

The Home screen summarizes today's expenses, month-to-date versus last month, and year-to-date versus last year. A projected full-year forecast and a rolling 12-month view give you a bird's-eye look at your financial health. The main currency is USD — you can add others in settings.

To log a new expense, just click Add, fill in a few fields, and save. If your Google Sheet has extra columns, you can set those up in QuickExpense too. Saving takes a moment — that's the Google API doing its thing — but it's a small price for data that stays fully in your sheet, under your control.

To search your history, just click here. By default, you'll see your most recent expenses. Start typing in Comments, and the list filters instantly, full search history. It's worth using comments for keeping a useful contact of a serviceman, a feedback on a purchase, or a model name so I can find details or spare parts later. The other fields are here too, and you can combine them — it's all matched with a logical AND. From this screen, you can also edit or delete any logged expense.

Now let's step back to initial setup, to explain quick start options. Here are two options, basically. 
First, pick your own Google Sheet, with your own expense categories. As QuickExpense requires a bare minimum of required columns, you may add custom currencies and extra columns on top right here, in the sheet configuration panel.
Second option, you may pick a predefined template sheet. It gets copied straight into your Google Drive automatically, containing built-in expense categories. It is the easiest way to start from scratch.
Also you can share a configured profile to other users, for example your family members.  

That's it. Feel free to try it. It's free. And your feedback is welcome.
